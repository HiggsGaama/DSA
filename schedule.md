# 24-Week Daily Pacing Calendar (1.5 – 2.0 Hours / Day)

> **Protocol**: Follow the strict 4-block timebox every study day:
> 15m Pre-Test $\rightarrow$ 25m Video Anchor $\rightarrow$ 50m Coding (1–2 problems) $\rightarrow$ 15m Flashcards.
> Days 1 to 5: New concepts & deliberate practice. Day 6: Timed contest / problem reinforcement. Day 7: Active recall review & rest.
