## 🔄 Spaced Repetition Review Protocol

To ensure retention over the 24-week period, every 4 weeks schedule a **Spaced Repetition Weekend**:
1. **Week 4 Review**: Re-solve 3 random problems from Topics 1 & 2 without viewing hints.
2. **Week 8 Review**: Re-solve 3 random problems from Topics 3 & 4.
3. **Week 12 Review**: Re-solve 4 random problems from Topics 5, 6, & 7.
4. **Week 16 Review**: Re-solve 4 random problems from Topics 8, 9, & 10.
5. **Week 20 Review**: Re-solve 4 random problems from Topics 11 & 12.
6. **Week 24 Review**: Comprehensive mock technical rounds across all topics.
